What is this site?

This site preserves copies of records that have appeared in unrelated places, under unrelated names, and with no reliable account of their origin. Some were sent directly to the Archivist. Others were recovered from abandoned pages, damaged recordings, and personal notes.

The purpose of this registry is not to explain the material. It is to prevent it from disappearing.

NOX-000 / PRIVATE TRANSCRIPT / SOURCE: WITNESS 03

Final retained message

The following text was recovered from the last available message attributed to the unidentified witness. No subsequent correspondence is preserved.

I know someone is watching me. Not from the street. Not through the window. I check those things. The room can be empty and I still know. I hear things after everything else has stopped. A sound in the next room. A word I almost recognize. Sometimes my own voice, except I haven't spoken. I used to be able to finish a thought. Now I wait for it, like I'm waiting for someone else to tell me what comes next. I think something is thinking for me. N.O.X. is close. I don't know what that means. I only know I didn't write those letters down the first time. If this reaches anyone, keep a copy. Please keep a copy. [END OF AVAILABLE TRANSCRIPT]

Archive note: the original message is incomplete. The witness's whereabouts and later circumstances are unverified.
